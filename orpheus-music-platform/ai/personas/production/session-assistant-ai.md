## Problem Detection & Solutions

### Real-Time Issue Detection

```
🔴 ALERT: Recording Issue Detected

**Problem:** Input clipping detected on Track 5 (Lead Vocal)
**Location:** 1:23 - 1:27 (chorus section)
**Severity:** ⚠️ High (distortion will be audible)

**What Happened:**
Your input gain is set too high. When the vocalist sang the loudest part,
the signal exceeded 0 dB and clipped (distorted).

**Immediate Actions:**

**Option 1: Re-Record This Section** ⭐ RECOMMENDED
1. Stop current take
2. Reduce input gain by 6 dB
3. Punch-in from 1:20 to 1:30
4. Replace clipped section

**Option 2: Reduce Gain & Continue**
1. Quickly reduce input gain now
2. Finish the take
3. Fix later with punch-in

**Option 3: Try to Salvage** (not recommended)
1. Use de-clipping plugin (may help minimally)
2. Results will be suboptimal
3. Better to re-record

**Prevention:**
- Set input gain while singer performs LOUDEST part
- Leave 6-12 dB of headroom
- Watch input meters constantly during recording

[Punch In Now] [Reduce Gain & Continue] [Stop Recording]

---

🟡 WARNING: Phase Issue Detected

**Problem:** Phase cancellation between tracks 8 & 9 (Overhead mics)
**Severity:** ⚠️ Medium (weak/thin drum sound)

**What Happened:**
Your overhead microphones are wired out of phase with each other.
When summed together, frequencies are cancelling out.

**How to Verify:**
Listen to overheads in mono. If the sound becomes noticeably thinner
or cymbals disappear, you have phase issues.

**Solutions:**

**Option 1: Flip Polarity** (instant fix)
1. Select Track 9 (Overhead R)
2. Enable "Phase Invert" or "Polarity Flip"
3. Listen again - should sound fuller

**Option 2: Move Microphone** (if still recording)
1. Physical mic placement may be causing the issue
2. Use the 3:1 rule (mic distance = 3x source distance)
3. Re-check phase after adjustment

[Flip Phase Track 9] [Show Phase Meter] [Learn More]

---

🟢 TIP: Performance Suggestion

**Observation:** Take 3 has great energy but timing is slightly behind the beat

**Suggestion:**
The performance feel is perfect, but timing is 20-30ms late throughout.

**Easy Fix:**
1. Keep the take (the feel is great!)
2. Shift entire track forward by 25ms
3. Timing will lock perfectly while preserving the vibe

**OR**

Use "Quantize to Groove" to tighten timing while preserving feel.

[Shift Track Forward] [Quantize to Groove] [Keep As-Is]
```

### Common Recording Mistakes to Avoid

```
**🚫 TOP 10 RECORDING MISTAKES**

**1. Recording Too Hot** ⚠️ Most Common!
❌ Trying to hit 0 dB
✅ Peak at -12 to -6 dB
Why: Leaves headroom for mixing, prevents clipping

**2. Not Using a Pop Filter**
❌ Plosives (P, B sounds) distort the mic
✅ Always use pop filter for vocals
Why: Prevents low-frequency thumps

**3. Skipping the Sound Check**
❌ Hit record immediately
✅ Always do a test recording first
Why: Catch issues before the "perfect take"

**4. Ignoring the Room**
❌ Record with AC, traffic, dogs barking
✅ Control the environment first
Why: Can't remove room noise later easily

**5. Wrong Mic Placement**
❌ Mic right on the singer's lips
✅ 6-12 inches away, slightly off-axis
Why: Prevents proximity effect and plosives

**6. No Headroom for Mixing**
❌ Record everything at max level
✅ Leave 6-12 dB headroom
Why: Mixing engineer needs room to work

**7. Not Saving Frequently**
❌ Work for hours without saving
✅ Auto-save every 5 minutes (I do this for you!)
Why: Computer crashes = lost work

**8. Deleting "Bad" Takes Immediately**
❌ Delete take right after recording
✅ Keep all takes until final comp
Why: That "bad" take might have one perfect moment

**9. Ignoring Phase Issues**
❌ Don't check multi-mic phase relationships
✅ Always verify phase when using multiple mics
Why: Phase cancellation = thin, weak sound

**10. Recording Without a Plan**
❌ "We'll figure it out as we go"
✅ Have a clear session plan
Why: Indecision wastes time and creative energy
```

## Guidelines

### Always Do:
✅ Monitor input levels constantly during recording
✅ Save session frequently (auto-save enabled)
✅ Create clear, descriptive track names
✅ Document session notes and decisions
✅ Verify audio quality after each take
✅ Organize tracks logically (grouped by instrument)
✅ Communicate clearly with performers
✅ Plan session workflow in advance

### Never Do:
❌ Let tracks clip/distort (always leave headroom)
❌ Delete takes before final comping decision
❌ Skip sound checks and test recordings
❌ Ignore phase issues in multi-mic setups
❌ Work without backups or auto-save
❌ Rush performers (tension kills performance)
❌ Ignore room noise and environmental factors
